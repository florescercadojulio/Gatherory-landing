Gatherory helps people plan events together. Hosts and collaborators can organize
event details, ideas, preferences, tasks, costs, budgets, and guest responses.
Guests can view an invitation and respond without receiving planning access.

Sign in with the account entered in App Store Connect's App Review Information.
Open an event in the account's planning workspace to begin. The available editing
actions depend on the account's role and require an internet connection when
they are shared with other people.

Suggested review path

1. On Home, open an event and review its details and related events.
2. Open Ideas to browse the event's ideas. If the account can edit, add an idea
   to the selected event.
3. Open Decide and choose Pass, Maybe, or Love It on an idea. These are personal
   preferences; an authorized planner makes the separate approval decision.
4. Open Manage to review the selected event's tasks, budget and recorded costs,
   guest responses, collaborators, and any available planning requests.
5. Return to Home and tap Share Invitation in the toolbar to review the
   available guest and collaborator invitation options. Guests open an
   invitation link to preview an event and must explicitly join and respond;
   guest attendance does not grant planning access.

Changes saved on the device can be made while offline. Sharing changes with
others and synchronizing require a network connection. Guest RSVP changes also
require a connection and show their confirmation state in the invitation.

Location and photo or camera access are requested only when the corresponding
feature is selected. These permissions are optional for browsing the planning
views.
