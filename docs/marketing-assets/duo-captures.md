# iPhone Duo screenshots

Captured October 21, 2026 from the native Gatherory iOS app in the iPhone Duo
simulator, using the isolated Maya’s Birthday Dinner marketing fixture. These
screens are in English and show fictional sample planners and plans. They are
simulator captures, not physical-device or release acceptance evidence.

The source PNGs are complete simulator screenshots copied without editing.
Ideas and Decide are from `/tmp/gatherory-duo-visuals/`; Manage was recaptured
on the isolated `Gatherory-Manage-Marketing` iOS 27.1 simulator. Each WebP is
encoded at quality 88 without resizing or cropping. No screen content was
composited, generated, or replaced.
The native layout checkpoint and fixture isolation details are in
[iphone-duo.md](../../../ios-apps/gatherory/docs/iphone-duo.md).
